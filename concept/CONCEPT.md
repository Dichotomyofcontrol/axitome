# Axitome redesign concept: The Ledger
(Concept mockup only. Not deployed. Branch axitome-redesign-concept.)

Axitome becomes a stone wall that gets one new line carved into it every day. The site already works this way (quote of the day counted from January 1, 2026, 744 lines on file); the redesign makes it visible.

## Five questions
1. Object: a stone wall of inscriptions that grows by one line a day.
2. Feel, beat by beat: stillness on arrival (travertine, nothing moving), weight as today's line is cut in word by word, the oxblood groove lands like a chisel strike, then lighter as you scroll the count, the themes and the last seven days, and leave with one line for tomorrow.
3. Anxiety: "another hustle-quote page that wants something." The answer, on screen the whole time: "One line. Nothing to buy."
4. Biggest claim: these words outlast us. Proof: the wall itself. The count of lines carved so far in Roman numerals, a ledger of past days, and every author page as a stack of dated slips.
5. Third click: each slip on an author page says which day it was carved (Day 31, Jan 31), and lines not yet carved say "Still on the shelf". Proposed next layer: hover a line for its chisel marks (source, theme, day carved).
