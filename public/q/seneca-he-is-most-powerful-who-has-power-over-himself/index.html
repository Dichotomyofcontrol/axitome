More on solitude

  • Would you have a great empire? Rule over yourself.

    Publilius Syrus

  • A king is surrounded by people,” Pascal wrote, “whose only thought is to divert him and stop him thinking about himself, because, king though he is, he becomes unhappy as soon as he thinks about himself.

    Blaise Pascal

  • Does getting upset provide me with more or fewer options? What is this worry blinding me to? What am I not seeing because I am choosing to worry? Does what happened keep me from acting with justice, with wisdom, moderation, or courage? What opportunity is contained within this situation? What might I get out of focusing on that instead? Isn’t it better that this happened to me instead of someone else—someone less equipped to handle it? What if I stopped and let this sit for a while? Would it resolve itself?
