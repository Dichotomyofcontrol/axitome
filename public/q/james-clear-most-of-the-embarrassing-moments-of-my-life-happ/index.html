  • If I could sink my teeth into the whole earth and actually taste it, I’d be happier for a moment… But I don’t always want to be happy. To be unhappy now and then is part of being natural. And so I take unhappiness with happiness naturally, just as I don’t marvel that there are mountains and plains and that there are rocks and grass… What matters is to be natural and calm in happiness and in unhappiness, to feel as if feeling were seeing, to think as if thinking were walking, and to remember, when death comes, that each day dies, and the sunset is beautiful, and so is the night that remains… That’s how it is and how I want it to be…

    Fernando Pessoa
